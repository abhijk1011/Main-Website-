Pure brass stitching wire

Pure brass stitching wire is made from a solid copper and zinc alloy, with no steel at all. There is no coating to wear off and nothing underneath to rust.

Applications

Where it is used

Many large packers use brass for all their export cartons, because it removes the risk of rust claims. The bright golden finish is a bonus.

Used for
Export cartons Pharma & food Retail facing Long sea legs

Sea export, coastal and monsoon storage, pharma, food and electronics packaging, and retail cartons where a rust mark is not acceptable.
