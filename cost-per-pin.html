Method

How it is calculated

The wire area is thickness multiplied by width. Multiply the area by the density of the metal (7.85 g/cm³ for mild steel, 8.50 for brass, 8.90 for copper) and by the length of one stitch to get the weight of one pin. Then multiply by the price per kilogram.

There is no allowance for scrap, wastage or margin. If you want to include these, add them to the price per kilogram.

Two tips. Measure the stitch length by straightening a stitch from your own machine rather than guessing. And replace the approximate prices with your actual quote, as metal prices change often, especially for copper and brass.
