Copper coated stitching wire

Mild steel wire with a copper coating. On a finished carton or a book spine it looks like solid copper at a much lower cost, and the smooth coating feeds easily through the machine.

Applications

Where it is used

Copper coated wire is not a rust protection grade. Where the copper coating is damaged, the steel underneath rusts faster, not slower. Choose copper coated wire for its look, and choose galvanised wire or a solid alloy where rust protection matters.

Used for
Presentation packaging Book work Retail facing cartons

Gift and retail packaging, books and binding, and any carton where the stitch can be seen by the customer and the box is stored dry.
