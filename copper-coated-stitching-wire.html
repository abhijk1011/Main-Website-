Copper coated stitching wire.

A mild steel core electroplated with copper. On a finished carton or a bound spine it reads as solid copper at a fraction of the cost, and the plating gives a smooth, low-drag feed through the head.

Where it belongs

What it is drawn for.

What it is not is a corrosion grade. Copper is noble to steel, so where the plating is breached the steel beneath corrodes faster rather than slower — the opposite of what zinc does. Specify copper coated for how it looks, and specify zinc or a solid alloy for how long it has to last.

Applications
Presentation packaging Book work Retail-facing cartons

Presentation and retail-facing packaging, book and bindery work, and any carton where the closure is visible to the end customer and the box is stored dry.
