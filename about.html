About us.

Prime Wires Private Limited is a leading manufacturer and exporter of high-quality Mild Steel (M.S.) wires and stitching wires, supplying a wide range of industrial applications across India and international markets. With a strong commitment to quality, consistency and customer satisfaction, we have established ourselves as a trusted partner for businesses seeking reliable wire solutions.

Based in Vasai, Palghar, Maharashtra, our manufacturing facility is equipped to produce an extensive range of wire products that meet stringent industry standards. Under the leadership of Mr. Bhavya Shah, Prime Wires continues to strengthen its position in the market through innovation, technical expertise and operational excellence.

Since 2003, for more than twenty years, we have been manufacturing world-class stitching wires, G.I. wires and M.S. wires at our own wire drawing plant. We have always put the quality of the product first and made it to last, and our wire is used by industry all over India and exported to customers in Europe, Africa and Asia.

Position

Why we draw our own wire.

Much of the stitching wire sold in India is bought in finished, re-spooled, and sold under a brand. We start at rod instead, so when a customer calls with a feed problem, we can trace it back to the cast, the draw and the bath for that exact reel number, and fix it at the operation that caused it.

It also means we can say no. If a carton is going to spend three months on a dock in Kochi, we will tell you galvanised is the wrong specification even though galvanised is the easier sale.

Export

Packed for the sea leg, not the truck.

Our wire is used all over India and exported to customers in Europe, Africa and Asia. Container condensation is the single biggest cause of rust claims on stitched cartons, and it happens long after the wire decision was signed off.

Export consignments are supplied on sealed reels, VCI-wrapped where the grade calls for it, palletised, strapped and shrink-wrapped, with mill test certificates and packing lists issued against reel numbers so a query four months later is still traceable.

For any carton crossing the equator we will generally recommend brass or copper regardless of what the enquiry asked for, and explain why. A container heats and cools through a voyage; moisture settles on the coldest metal inside it; galvanised stitches are frequently the first thing to mark. The claim arrives a quarter later, long after the wire saving was booked.

Documentation we issue as standard: commercial invoice, packing list, mill test certificate, certificate of origin on request, and reel-level traceability against the lot record.
