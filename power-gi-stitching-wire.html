Power G.I. stitching wire

Power G.I. stitching wire carries the heaviest zinc coating we put on steel wire. It is the most rust resistant of our galvanised grades.

Applications

Where it is used

Use it for coastal warehouses and long transit, where R.P. wire is not enough but brass would cost more than the carton justifies. For sea export, see pure brass.

Used for
Coastal warehousing Long inland transit Extended storage

Coastal and humid warehouses, long transit, long storage before dispatch, and high speed lines where rust problems cause rework and downtime.
