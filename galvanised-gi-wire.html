Steel wires

G.I. wire

G.I. wire is mild steel wire with a zinc coating. The zinc protects the steel from rust, even where the coating gets scratched, which is why galvanised wire lasts outdoors and plain wire does not.

Applications

Where it is used

The coating class follows IS 4826 and is stated on the test certificate. A heavier coating gives a longer life outdoors.

Used for
Fencing Wire mesh & netting Cables Stitching & binding wire Imitation jewellery Engineering work

Fencing, wire mesh and netting, cables, stitching and binding wire, imitation jewellery and general engineering work. Available hot dip or electro galvanised, with an even zinc coating and a bright finish.
