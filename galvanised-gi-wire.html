Steel Wire 07 · Zinc coated

G.I. wire.

Mild steel wire carrying a zinc coating. Zinc is anodic to steel, so it corrodes first and goes on protecting the steel underneath even where the coating has been scratched through — which is the whole reason galvanised wire survives outdoors and bright wire does not.

Where it belongs

What it is drawn for.

Coating class is specified to IS 4826 and stated on the test certificate. How many years the wire buys you is a function of that number and very little else, which is why we publish it.

Applications
Fencing Welded mesh Outdoor & agricultural Armouring General industrial

Fencing and chain link, welded mesh, outdoor and agricultural use, cable armouring, and general industrial applications where the wire is exposed to weather or to uncontrolled storage.
