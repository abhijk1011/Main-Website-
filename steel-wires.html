Steel wires

Every wire in this range is drawn at our own plant in Waliv, from wire rod to finished coil. We make mild steel wire in hard, half hard and soft (annealed) grades, high carbon wire for springs and ropes, and zinc coated G.I. wire. Full specifications are given for each product.

Group 1

Mild steel wires

H.B. wire, H.H.B. wire, annealed wire, binding wire and baling wire. The same steel, made in five different grades for different uses.

01 · Hard drawn, bright finish

H.B. wire (hard bright)

H.B. wire is low carbon mild steel wire, cold drawn through dies with no annealing after the last pass. This keeps the wire stiff and strong, and leaves the surface bright and clean.

02 · Half hard, formable

H.H.B. wire (half hard bright)

H.H.B. wire is the same low carbon steel as H.B. wire, annealed after drawing to a half hard condition. It is softer and easier to shape, so it does not crack when nails, screws and rivets are cold headed from it.

04 · Soft wire for tying

Binding wire

Binding wire is soft annealed mild steel wire used to tie rebar on building sites and for general bundling. We keep the size within close limits, because thin wire snaps when twisted and thick wire wastes metal on every tie.

05 · For baling

Baling wire

Baling wire, also called bale wire or soft wire, is annealed mild steel wire used to tie bales of cardboard, paper, textiles, aluminium and other recycled material. A bale tie stays under load for months and takes a shock each time the bale is moved, so the wire needs good stretch as well as strength.

High carbon wire

High carbon wire is made from high carbon steel that is patented (heat treated) and cold drawn to high strength. It has two to four times the carbon of mild steel, so it springs back to shape under load instead of staying bent.

07 · Zinc coated

G.I. wire

G.I. wire is mild steel wire with a zinc coating. The zinc protects the steel from rust, even where the coating gets scratched, which is why galvanised wire lasts outdoors and plain wire does not.

Standards

Standards we follow

IS 280 : 2006
Mild steel wire for general engineering purposes. Covers chemistry, diameter tolerance, tensile and the wrapping test for H.B., H.H.B., annealed, binding and baling wire, and for the base wire under G.I.
IS 4826 : 1979
Hot dipped galvanized coatings on round steel wires. Sets minimum coating mass by diameter for light, medium and heavy classes, plus adhesion and uniformity testing.
IS 4454 (Part 1) : 2001
Steel wire for mechanical springs, patented and cold drawn, unalloyed. Sets the SL / SM / SH and DM / DH grades and their tensile bands by diameter.
