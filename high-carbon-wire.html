Steel Wire 06 · Patented and cold drawn

High carbon wire.

Unalloyed high carbon steel, patented and cold drawn to tensile. This is a different steel from everything else in the Steel Wires range. The carbon runs two to four times that of mild steel, and the wire is worked to hold a load and return to shape rather than to bend and stay bent.
