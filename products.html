The range

Six grades of stitching wire, and the steel wire behind them.

Everything below is manufactured at our own plant at Waliv, from rod intake to sealed reel. Sections run 20 to 26 gauge flat; spools from 2 kg for hand work to 25 kg for the automatics. Rust resistant stitching wire

The trade calls it R.P. wire. Identical steel core to G.I., with a materially heavier zinc deposit that pushes back the onset of surface rust — for monsoon-season runs and uncontrolled inland storage.

06 · Solid copper, softest clinch

Pure copper stitching wire

Solid drawn electrolytic copper. Like brass it cannot rust, and its lower work-hardening gives a softer, more forgiving clinch — useful on thick multi-wall board and on older heads where crown deformation is a problem.

Bindery

Book & narrow flat stitching wire

Saddle stitching, side stitching and spiral work, in 24 to 26 gauge on 2 and 5 kg spools. Available in galvanised, copper coated, brass and pure copper.

A narrow flat section gives a broader crown bearing on the spine than round wire of the same mass, which means less cut-through on thin cover stock. Many binders outside India have moved to flat for exactly this reason. It needs a head rated for flat wire.
