Steel wires

H.B. wire (hard bright)

H.B. wire is low carbon mild steel wire, cold drawn through dies with no annealing after the last pass. This keeps the wire stiff and strong, and leaves the surface bright and clean.

Applications

Where it is used

H.B. wire is the base wire for many other wire products, including our own G.I. wire and binding wire.

Used for
Galvanising & copper coating Construction Spring manufacturing Nail manufacturing Fasteners Welded mesh Paint rollers Display & showcase stands

Used in galvanising, copper coating, construction and spring making. Also used for nails, fasteners, welded mesh, paint rollers, display stands and general engineering parts.
