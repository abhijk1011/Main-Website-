Steel Wire 01 · Hard drawn, bright finish

H.B. wire (hard bright).

Low carbon mild steel rod, descaled and drawn cold through successive dies with no anneal after the final pass. The wire keeps every bit of the work hardening the draw puts into it, which is where its stiffness and its tensile come from, and pickling before drawing leaves the surface bright rather than scaled.

Where it belongs

What it is drawn for.

It is the base wire for a large part of what the Indian wire industry makes downstream, ours included. Our own G.I. and binding wire start here.

Applications
Nail manufacturing Fasteners Welded mesh Paint rollers Display & showcase stands

Nail manufacturing, fasteners, welded wire mesh, paint rollers, showcase and display stands, cycle spokes and general engineering components, and as base wire for galvanised and binding wire.
