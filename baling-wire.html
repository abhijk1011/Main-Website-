Baling wire

Baling wire, also called bale wire, farm wire or soft wire, is annealed mild steel wire used to tie bales of cardboard, paper, textiles, aluminium and other material for recycling. It has even tensile strength and elongation, so it ties a strong knot with very little stretch.
