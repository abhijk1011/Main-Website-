Stitching wires

We make flat stitching wire for corrugated boxes, books, cheque books, leaflets and printed work, in seven grades. Sizes run from 20 to 26 gauge, on spools from 2 kg for hand and bindery work up to 25 kg for automatic machines.

We use good quality raw material and modern galvanising and flattening machines, and we draw our own wire from rod, so the size stays the same from the start of a reel to the end. Every reel is checked during production and again before dispatch, to keep rejections close to zero. Every grade runs on manual, semi automatic and fully automatic stitching machines such as Insun, Gods Will and MEPL.

01 · Most popular

G.I. stitching wire

G.I. stitching wire is mild steel wire coated with zinc and rolled flat. It is the stitching wire most used by corrugated box makers in India, and the best value choice for cartons that are kept dry and opened within a few weeks.

02 · Heavier zinc coating

Rust resistant stitching wire

Rust resistant stitching wire, known in the trade as R.P. wire, has the same steel core as G.I. wire but a much heavier zinc coating. It resists rust for longer, which suits the monsoon season, storage without humidity control and cartons that are stored for months before they ship.

Stainless steel stitching wire

07 · Softest clinch

Pure copper stitching wire

Pure copper stitching wire is made from solid electrolytic copper. Like brass, it cannot rust because it contains no iron. Over time it darkens to a natural patina, which stays on the surface and does not stain the carton. It costs about the same as brass.

Bindery

Book stitching wire

Round wire used by the printing and notebook industry to bind books, leaflets, catalogues and cheque books, and for spiral and Wire O binding. Supplied on spools or in coils, as you need.

It has a bright finish and even tensile strength, and runs on manual, semi automatic and fully automatic book stitching machines such as LineOmatic, Bielomatik, Muller Martini, ECH Will and JoyDzign.

Exercise books Notebooks Textbooks Duplicate and triplicate books Magazines Catalogues Cheque books

Narrow flat stitching wire

We also make narrow flat wire for book stitching. Many book binders and printers abroad use flat wire because it grips a wider area of the spine, is less likely to cut through thin covers, and gives a binding that lasts longer. Your stitching head must be suitable for flat wire, so please check before ordering.
