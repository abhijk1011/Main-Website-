Steel Wire 04 · Soft annealed, for tying

Binding wire.

Soft annealed mild steel wire for rebar tying and general bundling. Gauge is held tight for a practical reason at both ends of the tolerance: wire that runs thin snaps in the twist and costs a man on site his rhythm, and wire that runs thick wastes metal on every single tie across a slab.

Applications
RCC rebar tying Construction reinforcement Bundling Fabrication

Reinforced concrete rebar tying on slabs, beams, columns and footings; general construction binding; fabrication and workshop bundling; and packaging where a soft, hand-workable tie is wanted.
