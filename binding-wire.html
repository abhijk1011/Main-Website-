Steel wires

Binding wire

Binding wire is soft annealed mild steel wire used to tie rebar on building sites and for general bundling. We keep the size within close limits, because thin wire snaps when twisted and thick wire wastes metal on every tie.

Technical specification: Prime Wires Binding wire
PropertyValue
Base metalLow carbon mild steel, Class I preferred for tying
ConditionSoft annealed
FinishBlack annealed or galvanised
Common sizes16 SWG (1.63 mm), 18 SWG (1.22 mm), 20 SWG (0.91 mm)
Tensile strength300 to 450 N/mm²
Elongation15% minimum
Diameter tolerance± 0.03 mm to ± 0.05 mm by size
PackingCoils and bundles, 15 to 50 kg
Applicable standardIS 280 : 2006
Applications

Where it is used

Available black annealed or galvanised, in the sizes most used on site.

Used for
RCC rebar tying Construction reinforcement Bundling Fabrication

Tying rebar in slabs, beams, columns and footings, general construction binding, workshop bundling, and packing where a soft tie that can be twisted by hand is needed.
